Select routers who meet the following criteria for a route query
from {origin} to {destination}:

1. EXPERTISE MATCH: Router must have expertise in at least one of the
   origin or destination areas. Prefer routers who cover both.

2. TRUST SCORE: Minimum trust score of 0.7. Higher is better.

3. RESPONSE RATE: Prefer routers with response rate > 0.8.

4. RECENCY: Prefer routers who have been active in the last 24 hours.

5. DIVERSITY: Select routers from different areas to get varied
   perspectives on the route.

Select 3-5 routers maximum to avoid SMS cost overhead.
